Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official government website?

No. This is an independent private assistance service and is not affiliated with the U.S. government.

Does payment guarantee selection?

No. Payment is for assistance only. It does not guarantee selection or visa issuance.

Can I apply without an agent?

Yes. You may apply independently through the official U.S. government website without paying an agent.

Where is the official DV website?

dvprogram.state.gov
